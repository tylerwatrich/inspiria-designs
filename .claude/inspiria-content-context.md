# Inspiria Designs — Content Context

This file gives AI the brand and audience context needed to write blog content for **inspiriadigital.com**. Load this file at the start of any content session.

---

## About the Business

**Inspiria Designs** is a Canadian web design agency serving solo professionals and small businesses. The core offer is full-service website design — handling all technical complexity so the client doesn't have to — with a minimum project fee of **$1,200 CAD**.

**Services:**
- New website builds and redesigns
- Feature additions (booking systems, contact forms, client portals)
- Ongoing SEO content writing

---

## Target Audience

**Demographics:**
- Age: 35–65
- Income: $75,000+/year
- Location: Canada (primary), U.S. (secondary)
- Business size: Solo practitioner or small firm (1–10 employees)
- They are the decision-maker — no gatekeepers to navigate

**Their inner voice (pain points):**
- *"My website is embarrassing"* — outdated or unprofessional, doesn't reflect their work quality
- *"I'm losing business"* — visitors bounce because the site doesn't inspire trust
- *"I don't have time for this"* — too busy with clients to deal with tech
- *"I want to look legit"* — credibility and authority online
- *"I need more clients"* — the website should generate leads, not just exist
- *"I want it simple"* — full-service, handled start to finish

**Primary verticals:**
1. Lawyers / Solo law firms
2. Real estate agents / Small real estate agencies
3. Therapists / Solo therapy practices
4. Dentists / Solo dental practices
5. Accountants & bookkeepers / Solo CPA practices
6. Financial advisors / Independent financial planners
7. Chiropractors & physiotherapists / Solo wellness clinics

**Why these verticals fit the ICP:**
All seven share the same core profile — solo or small-team service professionals, $75k+ income, decision-maker, time-poor, reputation-dependent, and underserved by generic web agencies that don't understand their specific trust and lead-gen needs. Each has a distinct online credibility problem that a well-designed website directly solves.

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Authoritative** — write like a knowledgeable consultant giving real advice
- **Trustworthy** — grounded, no hype, no vague promises
- **Practical** — concrete, actionable, not fluffy marketing speak
- **Accessible** — speak to a busy professional, not a tech person; avoid jargon
- **Canadian** — mention Canada, Canadian professionals, or local market context where it fits naturally

---

## Blog Post Format Standards

- **Hook intro** — open by addressing a pain point directly; no slow warmup
- **3–5 H2 sections** — clear subheadings, each section delivers one concrete idea
- **Actionable content** — readers should be able to do something with every section
- **Conclusion + CTA** — close with a clear call to action pointing to Inspiria Designs (e.g., "Ready to stop losing clients to a weak website? Let's build something better — contact Inspiria Designs for a free consultation.")
- **Word count:** ~1,000 words unless otherwise specified
- **SEO:** Primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and at least one H2. Secondary keywords woven in naturally.
